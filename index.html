APL Reports & Financials: Dashboards, Wins & Challenges

Ninety minutes, thirty per market. Each presents its monthly dashboard for a total picture of the business, then walks its biggest wins and the why behind them and its two or three biggest challenges, with the floor open for the group's counsel.

  • Austin: month six of the new operations structure, operational KPIs taking root across divisions, the marketing transition under Greg, and where the call volume challenge stands now
  • Dallas: finalized financials and fixed-asset work for true profitability, where the 2027 budget stands with the year winding down, and department-specific growth targets
  • Houston: best profit year on record, commercial carrying growth, Orlando expansion, and the Salesforce conversion reset
  • Format per market: dashboard and financials first, then the three biggest wins and the two or three biggest challenges, then open counsel and cross-pollination from the table

7:30 - 9:30 PM

Centralization Discovery: Where Does Shared Scale Make Sense?

A discovery conversation, not a commitment. Ground rule: even learning what each market does differently counts as a win, and this conversation deserves a regular seat at the ranch. The frame that holds it together: shared assets get shared strategy; independent operations stay independent by choice, not by drift.

  • The website, our one shared asset: findings from the audit and third-party consultation, a unified SEO effort and who leads it, and whether our current vendors can take us to the next revenue tier
  • Marketing: the Forshaw precedent, economies of scale, and the shared-CMO question
  • Fleet: leasing versus owning, buying power, and what each market has learned
  • Back of house: insurance and benefits, accounting, HR, and purchasing: where redundancy costs us
9:30 - 10:30 PM

Intergenerational Investment

Thirty minutes on the year's leadership development arc, with most of the time on the capstone rather than the review. The annual meeting is weeks out, not quarters.

  • What the three quarters built: where managers are visibly different than in January, and where the training has not moved the needle yet

Houston's read on it, and a group decision on how they come into the capstone without asking Austin and Dallas to sit through a year they have already done
